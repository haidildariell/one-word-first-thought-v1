This entry is a small Python program (`main.py`, stdlib only) that writes exactly one word to `/output/entry.txt`. The word is **defenestration**.

Why this word fits the judge's criteria: the field is saturated with dreamy nature words, so nobody guesses a word about throwing someone out of a window — that is pure surprise. It is also vivid and specific: a person flying through a window is an instant cinematic image, not a vague feeling. And it carries a spark of wit — absurdly specific, fun to say, and it works both literally and as a metaphor for being thrown out of a room, a job, or a conversation.
